MILITARY DISCOUNT PROGRAM

Corvette Central extends our gratitude to those military members who have served and those in active service to our country. As a token of our appreciation, Corvette Central offers a 10% discount to all current and former Military customers. Please send your email request to customerservice@corvettecentral.com along with a copy of your DD214 form or some other proof of service to enroll.

Price Match Guarantee

Corvette Central will match any Corvette Competitor’s current advertised price on an identical product. Simply send us a copy of the competitor’s catalog or ad showing the current advertised price. The product must be from the same source or vendor. Note: This does not include sale prices or web specials. Cannot be combined with any other discount or special offer.

*(ND) - NO DISCOUNT

An (ND) within the description of a product means there is no discount available, regardless of any offer.
