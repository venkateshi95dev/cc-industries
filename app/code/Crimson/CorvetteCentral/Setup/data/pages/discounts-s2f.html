START-TO-FINISH PROGRAM

Corvette Central's Start-to-Finish Program offers our customers doing a partial or complete restoration the best price, service, and selection for their project needs.

  • Orders placed will receive a 5-15% discount on most items. Items with (ND) in the title are non-discountable items and do not qualify.
  • Maintain your preferred customer status by spending only $500.00 each year.  
  • To enroll, please inquire with our order department and place an initial order of $2500.00 or more. The initial order will receive the Start-to-Finish discount.

All pricing and discounts are based on current list prices and are subject to change without notice. You may not combine your Start-to-Finish discount with any other offers or programs.
