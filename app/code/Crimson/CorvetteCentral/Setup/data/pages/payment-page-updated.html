Payment Methods

We accept all major Credit Cards for payment on retail orders: MasterCard, Visa, Discover, or American Express. (Please note: American Express is not accepted for payment on wholesale orders.)

Paypal - Accepted on internet orders placed on our website.

Wire Transfers - Preferred method for large international orders. Please contact our order department for more information.

If you prefer to mail in an order with a check or money order: Please call our order department at 800-345-4122 or 269-426-3342 during normal business hours for a quote on your order, including shipping and any applicable sales tax. This will ensure that you mail in the correct amount with your order. U.S. money orders or 1st party imprinted checks only. Orders prepaid by check are held for 7-10 business days. A $25.00 fee is charged for each return of a check.
