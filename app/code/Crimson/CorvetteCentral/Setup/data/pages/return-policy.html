RETURN POLICY

Corvette Central wants you to have a 100% satisfying experience shopping with us. We understand that sometimes, things go wrong with a delivery. When your order arrives, inspect all of your packages immediately. Call us at 1-800-345-4122 or email customerservice@corvettecentral.com for assistance in the following cases:

DAMAGED

  • Email photos of the damage. Do not discard the packaging
  • We are not responsible for UPS-delivered claims after 30 days

DEFECTIVE

  • Report defective items within 30 days of receipt
  • Items will be repaired or replaced at the vendor's discretion

MISSING

  • Report any missing items within 30 days of receipt
  • We will process a replacement of the part

INCORRECT

  • Report any incorrect items within 30 days of receipt
  • We will process an exchange for the part

Products that have not been installed, altered, or damaged can be returned to Corvette Central.  After returns are received and inspected, a refund will be processed. Follow the steps below, or a 15% restocking fee will apply.  

Core returns: All cores are inspected upon return and may be subject to the vendor's approval before a refund is issued. Cores must be complete and rebuildable to be eligible for a full refund.

1. COMPLETE FORM

  • Fill out the form on the invoice and include it in the box  
  • Provide the reason for the return

2. PACK

  • Pack the item to prevent damage
  • We are not responsible for damage incurred during shipping

3. INSURE

  • Insure your return package with the shipping company
  • We recommend UPS
